Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Inklith is a writing workspace designed to help authors organize, write, and develop their stories.

Information We Collect

Inklith only collects information that is necessary for features that users choose to use. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the public website.

Website

The Inklith website is primarily used to provide information about the application and distribute the Android application package.

Application Data

Information created by you inside Inklith may include writing projects, notes, characters, worldbuilding information, and other creative content. The way this information is stored depends on the features and version of the application you use.

Third-Party Services

Inklith may use third-party services for hosting, distribution, infrastructure, or future optional services. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect Inklith and its infrastructure. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Children and Personal Information

Users should not submit sensitive personal information through Inklith unless a feature explicitly requires it and appropriate safeguards are provided.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Inklith gains new features or services. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact method provided on the official Inklith website or repository.
